# Forest surfaces, compact portrait and continuous journey

Founder-requested refinement, October 20, 2026. Applies to the existing
Portuguese/American English candidate in PR #28, without publication.

- Removed the language-availability note and the explanatory creations aside
  in both languages. The language selector itself remains.
- A shared experience summary now supplies the requested recruiting, research,
  learning and everyday-productivity text to the home and professional
  portfolio, with manual American English localization.
- The same portrait image is clipped with CSS into a strict circle, at most
  240px wide on desktop and 180px on mobile. No generated replacement, image
  processing or rectangular photo-card background is introduced. The hero
  uses a 2:1 text/portrait layout; work and education grids have asymmetric
  proportions and collapse into a readable single column on small screens.
- Source Sans 3 is now used throughout, with a true variable-font 700 heading
  weight and comfortable prose leading. It meets the requested humanist sans
  direction without another download/provider. Serif assets remain available
  for old references but are not used or preloaded by the current pages.
- Gray panels are replaced by forest-green surfaces. At desktop sizes,
  supporting browsers use a very light green tint at 4.5% opacity, a subtle
  translucent border and 6px backdrop blur. Unsupported browsers, mobile and
  reduced-transparency preferences use a solid forest fallback. Cards share
  20px corners; compact chips use 12px. The Reta Final artwork keeps its own
  navy/orange product identity.
- One static full-height vector background per main content region replaces
  disconnected decorations. A fine dashed route uses a mint/copper gradient
  at 20% opacity. Section-color layers sit at z-index -2, the continuous map
  at -1 and cards at 0. Background shapes are hidden from assistive technology
  and cannot capture pointer input. No scroll listener or animation is needed.

These references informed adaptation, not a copied layout or a usability
study of this site. The Founder's supplied screenshots identify the
specific existing-site areas being corrected. Contrast and browser assertions
are updated to the new design while retaining AA thresholds, overflow, text
spacing, no-JavaScript locale journeys and relay regressions. The privacy
policy's separately discussed substantive revision remains outside this edit.
