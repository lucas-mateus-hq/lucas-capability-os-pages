# Personal editorial identity

The Founder-supplied visual direction is applied to the eight existing public
pages, preserving approved Portuguese/American English content and navigation.

| Token | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark teal | `#071B20` | Dominant background and header |
| Ink charcoal | `#1F2933` | Cards and alternate editorial sections |
| Deep teal | `#0F6B61` | Contact section |
| Warm ivory | `#F7F3EA` | Primary text |
| Mint | `#73CDBA` | Personal-site buttons, links and focus |
| Warm copper | `#C58A45` | Section labels, contour routes and restrained emphasis |

Source Serif 4 regular 400 provides upright editorial headings. Source Sans 3
provides prose and interface text. Both use the existing self-hosted files;
no new font download or synthetic semibold serif face is introduced.

Low-opacity vector contour lines and a copper route are decorative only, with
no location, timeline or metric semantics. They are hidden from assistive
technology, have no animation and cannot intercept interaction. Distinct dark
section surfaces, card borders and copper section markers maintain separation
without large light backgrounds. Buttons are rounded, and the approved 14px
language flags retain their 44px touch targets.

The existing personal portrait and facts remain. Generated reference mockups
guide styling only; their person, biography, fictional articles, newsletter and
search control are not copied into the site. No new publication or product is
announced. Reta Final retains its navy/orange identity within its product card
and existing illustrative tablet artwork.

Static contrast pairs and actual browser theme assertions are updated to the
new design, retaining AA thresholds, overflow/spacing checks, locale journeys
and all regression gates. This is implementation validation, not a reader
study, formal accessibility audit or legal revision of the privacy policy.

The October 20 refinement in [VISUAL_POLISH_V4.md](VISUAL_POLISH_V4.md) supersedes the charcoal surfaces, serif headings and disconnected map treatment described above.
